# RULES.md

# 1. Core Engineering Principles

Follow these principles:

* SOLID
* DRY
* KISS
* Separation of Concerns
* Composition over Inheritance

## General Rules

* Prefer readability over cleverness.
* Keep functions small and focused.
* Use meaningful names.
* Avoid unnecessary comments.
* Do not introduce abstractions without a clear purpose.
* Avoid duplicate business logic.
* Keep responsibilities separated.

---

# 2. Final Rule

When unsure:

* Choose the simplest solution.
* Keep logic in the correct layer.
* Prefer maintainability over speed of implementation.
* Write code that another developer can understand without explanation.
